# v0.6.54 — Check your audio before going live

In **Streaming Destinations**, choose what to **Send to stream**. Speak into your microphone or play a scene source and watch the **Stream audio** meter. The meter shows the final Program mix going into the encoder.

Use **Open Audio Mixer** to select a microphone or interface under **Input settings**, unmute its line, or raise **Program Master**. You can adjust the mix during a stream. Muting, unmuting, changing volume and routing an on-air source now apply immediately.

Audio and video files now feed Program directly through the mixer. The app checks for a live Program audio track before starting the encoder. An intentional **Mute all** still allows streaming and you can bring the sound back during the broadcast. Device and capture errors now use styled alerts with recovery guidance.

Update through **Menu → Check for Updates**, or download the installer from the [release page](https://github.com/WealthGate/DualCast-Studio/releases/tag/v0.6.54).

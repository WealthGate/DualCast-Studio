# v0.6.47 — Smoother scene switching

Program keeps showing the outgoing scene until the incoming scene has a usable picture. This prevents a black flash when a camera, video or browser source takes longer to load. If a scene has no available picture, the operator can choose another scene while Program holds its last picture.

The default transition is a 400 ms fade. Select **Cut** for an immediate switch once the incoming picture is ready. The outgoing video keeps moving during the fade when its source is available. A switch made mid-transition continues from the current picture.

Program Focus keeps the **LIVE** badge on the outgoing scene until the switch finishes. Browser sources wait for their pages to load before supplying a frame.

In the installed app, choose **Menu → Check for Updates**, download v0.6.47, then choose **Restart & Install**. Windows, macOS and Linux installers are available on the [v0.6.47 release page](https://github.com/WealthGate/DualCast-Studio/releases/tag/v0.6.47).

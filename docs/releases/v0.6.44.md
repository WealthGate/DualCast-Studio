# OpenChurch Broadcast Studio v0.6.44

Run a service with fewer steps: show lower-third text without a logo, clear the previous service's slides, add content to a schedule from its editor, and click a scheduled item to display it immediately.

- **Text Only (No Logo):** one button beside **All outputs** shows text while hiding the standard or Bible lower-third logo. It works in Classic and Designer modes on Preview, Program, mirrored displays, recordings/streams, and the separate lower-third output. Independent sanctuary text remains visible. Choose **Show Text** to restore the configured logo. The mobile remote includes the same text-only option.
- **Clear All Slides:** clear the complete slide queue and both Preview/Program text cues, with confirmation. Saved schedules, downloaded songs, Bible libraries, and source media remain available. Failed saves restore the queue; delayed Scripture lookups cannot refill a cleared queue.
- **Add to Schedule from the editor:** add typed announcements, songs/lyrics, saved songs, edited slides, Designer cards, selected or typed Scripture references, displayed Scripture, scenes, and sources directly. Choose the destination service beside the button; the first addition can create a service. Adding content retains its draft and leaves live output unchanged.
- **Drag to order the service:** use each item's grip to move it, with a visible drop target. Mouse and touch/pen handlers are included; up/down buttons remain available. Changes save automatically without changing the live slide.
- **Click to show a schedule item:** hymns and presentations begin on their first slide; Scripture appears in its editor and the lower third. Scheduled text reaches sanctuary displays while retaining their independent layout/background. Scene items restore their saved scene. Private notes remain private, and **Edit** changes details without going live.
- Fix saved schedules not refreshing after settings load, frozen/black/blended output masking schedule playback, stale live indicators after clearing, and Scripture source markup in scheduled references. A full slide queue now gives an actionable message instead of silently losing the new presentation during save.
- Update Electron to 42.11.16 and patch production/build dependencies identified by the audit. The final dependency audit reports zero known advisories.

**Get the update:** select **Menu → Check for Updates**, download **v0.6.44**, then choose **Restart & Install**, or download an installer below.

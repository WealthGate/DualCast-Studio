# v0.6.52 — Drag floating panels back into the workspace

Drag a floating panel by its header. The workspace highlights the area under your pointer.

- Drop on **Dock top**, **Dock left**, **Dock right**, or **Dock bottom** to add a separate dock there.
- Drop on **Tab here** to join an existing panel group. Use a neighboring drop target to split above, below, left or right.
- On scrollable workspaces, hold your pointer near the top or bottom while dragging to reach docks outside the visible area.
- Release away from the targets to move the panel while keeping it floating. Hold **Ctrl** on Windows or Linux or **Command** on macOS to keep it floating anywhere. The return arrow still takes it back to its previous dock.

Docking preserves the panel's open controls and saves its placement for the next session.

Update through **Menu → Check for Updates** or get an installer from the [v0.6.52 release page](https://github.com/WealthGate/DualCast-Studio/releases/tag/v0.6.52).

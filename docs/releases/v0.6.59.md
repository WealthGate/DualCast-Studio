# v0.6.59 — Smoother mirrored video and a clearer schedule selector

When playing a video during a stream, a mirrored sanctuary display now encodes at its selected frame rate even when the stream requests frames more often. Matching resolutions skip an intermediate canvas copy, and the sanctuary window draws the decoded picture directly. Your output resolution, frame rate and encoding quality settings are preserved.

The destination selector beside **Add to Schedule** now matches the studio theme, with a visible arrow and keyboard focus indicator. It wraps beneath the button when a dock is narrow.

Choose **Menu → Check for Updates** and restart to install after the service.

# v0.6.50 — Find service content and choose a workspace theme

- Open **Find in Service** or press **Ctrl+K** on Windows and Linux or **Command+K** on macOS. Search schedules, sermon slides, Scripture, local song lyrics and scenes. Search by words on a slide to jump to that slide. Use **Cue in Preview** to prepare it privately, or select **Show live** to put it on air. Operator notes stay private.
- Type a Scripture reference, such as `John 3:16–18`, to look it up with the selected Bible source. With an offline library selected, the lookup works offline.
- Use **Panels** to locate or reopen workspace docks. Use the arrow keys to switch dock tabs. Switching tabs preserves your place in the controls.
- Choose one of eight themes or follow the system appearance under **Settings → Appearance**. Themes change the operator workspace.
- Open **VST effects** at the top of the Audio Mixer. On Windows x64, **Process Source Audio** runs the Program mix through one VST2 or VST3 effect. **Replace Source Audio** uses a receiver such as ReaStream as the Program input.
- Review the Terms and Conditions in interactive Windows setup and the macOS disk image. If your profile has yet to accept the version, the app prompts when it opens and saves acceptance locally. Updates wait for **Restart & Install** and protect active streams and recordings from an update restart.

Update through **Menu → Check for Updates** or get an installer from the [v0.6.50 release page](https://github.com/WealthGate/DualCast-Studio/releases/tag/v0.6.50).

# v0.6.57 — Mobile Scripture, service cues and dock locking

On the phone or tablet, open **Scripture**, choose a book and chapter, then double-tap a verse number to show it in both Preview and Program. A single tap selects the reference privately. **Load into Preview** and **Show reference live** are also available. Offline translations use the books, chapters and verses installed on the studio computer.

Open **Service** to browse schedules in service order, search songs and hymns, or find prepared slides. Choose a slide and use **Stage in Preview** or **Show live**. Operator notes are read-only. Tap **Refresh** to pick up desktop edits.

On the desktop, **Panels → Lock dock layout** protects tab placement, dock sizes and floating sections. The setting survives a restart. Select **Unlock dock layout** to rearrange panels again.

Stream recovery now checks recent delivery progress during long broadcasts and reduces video load when delivery stays behind real time. Stopping a healthy high-bitrate stream allows buffered audio and video to finish sending. The selected resolution still depends on encoder capacity and upload bandwidth.

Choose **Menu → Check for Updates** on the studio computer, then restart to install. Reopen the same Tailscale remote address or Home Screen app on the phone to load the updated controls.

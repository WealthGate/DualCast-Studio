# v0.6.49 — Program mixer and vocal effects

- The larger **Program Master** fader and stereo meter stay visible while you scroll the Audio Mixer. Input lines have wide faders, meters, and individual mute buttons.
- Use **Add input line** to set up microphones and instruments independently. Each new line starts muted. Open **Input settings** to name the line and select a device or a numbered channel. The mixer supports eight lines, including the primary microphone. Available channels depend on the device and its driver.
- Choose **Radio Gold** for warm, even speech, **Lead Vocal** for clearer singing, **Choir** for real-time stereo doubling, or **Clean** for the original input. Each line saves its own preset, volume, and mute state. Effects run locally, and lines assigned to the same device share its capture.

To hear your lines, choose **Input lines (all scenes)** or **Scene sources + input lines** under **Program audio**, then unmute the lines you want on air.

Choose **Menu → Check for Updates**, download the update, and select **Restart & Install**. Installers are available on the [v0.6.49 release page](https://github.com/WealthGate/DualCast-Studio/releases/tag/v0.6.49).
